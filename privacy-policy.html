Privacy Policy

JSON Master for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

JSON Master collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs and no servers of ours. Your documents and your settings stay on your device and in storage you already control. The only connection the app makes is to Apple's App Store, for the optional JSON Master Pro purchase, and it carries nothing from your documents.

01Your documents

Documents are ordinary files — .json, .jsonc, .jsonl, .ndjson and .geojson — that you open and save through the system's own file browser: Files on iPhone and iPad, Finder on Mac. The app reads and writes only the file you have open, and only where you told it to. It never uploads a document, never copies one elsewhere and never scans your storage for other files.

Exports — formatted JSON, minified JSON and CSV — are written to the location you choose in the system save panel. The sample document offered in Settings is written into the app's own folder, and only when you ask for it.

If you keep files in iCloud Drive or in another provider connected to Files, syncing is done by that service, not by us, and is governed by its own privacy policy. The app itself performs no syncing.

02What you paste into it

The clipboard is read only when you paste — into a document, into the token decoder, or as the second document in Compare — and what you paste is processed on your device and nowhere else. Decoding a token, comparing documents, running a JSONPath query, repairing almost-JSON and generating Swift, TypeScript or JSON Schema types all happen locally, in the app's own code.

This matters most for tokens: the app splits a JWT and decodes its header and payload so you can read them. It does not verify the signature and does not contact any server to do so — the screen says as much while you are looking at it.

03Your settings

Your theme, text size, tree density, path notation, copying, indentation and parsing preferences are saved on your device in standard app preferences, together with whether JSON Master Pro is unlocked. The interface language is the system's own per-app language setting. These are technical settings: they contain nothing personal and never leave the device, apart from the device backups you make yourself.

04In-app purchase

JSON Master Pro is an optional one-time purchase sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to show the price in your currency, to make the purchase, to restore it, and to check with the App Store whether your Apple Account owns Pro. That exchange is between your device and Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; it contains nothing from your documents.

Apple gives developers anonymous, aggregated sales reports. They do not identify you, and we have no other record of who bought the app or the purchase.

05Network use

Parsing, editing, searching, querying, converting and exporting happen on your device, with or without an internet connection. The app has no servers of its own and sends your documents nowhere. Its only network traffic is the App Store purchase check described above, made by Apple's StoreKit. The only outbound links in the app are two entries in the Help menu, pointing at the JSON specification and the JSONPath RFC; clicking one opens your browser, and nothing is sent from the app either way.

06No analytics or tracking

There is no analytics framework, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party code of any kind in the app. We do not know who you are, whether you use the app or what you open with it. If you choose to share crash reports with Apple in your device settings, Apple may make them available to developers in anonymous form under Apple's own privacy policy.

07Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

08Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
